Camera and coffee sample images

These pixel-byte resources reproduce the corresponding public arrays from
scikit-image 0.26.0. Its pinned data function documentation records the
following asset-specific provenance and rights.

camera.gray
Camera photograph by Lav Varshney, dedicated under CC0 by the photographer.
Source in the distribution: skimage/data/camera.png.
This is the replacement image introduced in scikit-image 0.18.
https://github.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/issues/3927
