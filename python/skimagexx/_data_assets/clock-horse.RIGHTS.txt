Clock and horse sample images

These pixel-byte resources reproduce the corresponding public arrays from
scikit-image 0.26.0. Its pinned data function documentation records the
following asset-specific provenance and rights.

clock.gray
Motion-blurred clock by Stefan van der Walt, released by the photographer
into the public domain. Source in the distribution: skimage/data/clock_motion.png.

horse.bool
Horse silhouette by Andreas Preuss (marauder), CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://openclipart.org/detail/158377/horse-by-marauder
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Source in the distribution: skimage/data/horse.png. These are the final public
Boolean pixels after the original image compositing and conversion pipeline.
